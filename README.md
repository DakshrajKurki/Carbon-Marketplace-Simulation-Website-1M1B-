# 🌱 The Green Ledgers — Carbon Credit Marketplace Simulator

A web-based simulator that explains how carbon-credit trading works, backed by analysis of **5,000 carbon-trading transactions** and a Tableau analytics dashboard. Built by Team *The Green Ledgers* in the **1M1B Green Internship (Batch 7)**, run with AICTE, Salesforce India and SEMI.

## Why

Carbon markets (1 credit = 1 tonne of CO₂) are a key tool for cutting emissions, but real trading platforms are complex and hard for students and small businesses to understand. This project gives a simplified, hands-on simulation alongside real market data.

## What we built

- **Multi-page marketplace website** — Home, Trade, Portfolio and Analytics pages, with real-time cost calculations (including GST) and portfolio tracking; deployed on Netlify
- **Data analysis** of a 5,000-row Kaggle carbon-trading dataset (industry, fuel type, emissions vs. allowance, carbon price, credits traded, verification status, compliance cost)
- **Tableau dashboard** of market behaviour — average price ≈ $27.53/t, ~551,944 tCO₂ traded, balanced buy/sell activity, manufacturing as the dominant sector
- **Stakeholder survey** — 17 respondents, 92% positive rating, strong demand for verified projects
